Privacy Policy

App: Aircraft Dispatcher Prep ADX (iPhone, iPad and Android)
Developer: MD Shahariar Nazim Joy
Last updated: November 9, 2026

Summary: This app is private by design. Your study data stays on your device. We do not require an account, and we do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or any personal profile. The app has no user accounts and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not use advertising identifiers.

2. Data stored on your device

Your progress, answers, bookmarks, flagged questions, spaced-repetition schedule, mock-exam history, and preferences are stored locally on your device using the operating system's on-device app storage. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time Lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store on iPhone and iPad, and by Google Play on Android. The store handles your payment and manages your subscription; the app receives only the purchase status needed to unlock features. We do not receive your payment details, Apple ID or Google account. The stores' handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Local notifications and read-aloud

If you enable the optional study reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. The optional "read question aloud" feature uses your device's built-in text-to-speech. No notification or speech data is sent to any server by the app.

5. No data sold or shared

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal information, because we do not collect any.

6. Children's privacy

The app is intended for adults preparing for a professional certification and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, delete, or port. You control all on-device data directly: erase it via Settings → Reset all progress or by deleting the app. We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

8. Security

Your data remains within the app's private storage on your device, protected by the operating system.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; material changes will be reflected here with a new date.

Questions about this policy? Email dmjks120@gmail.com.

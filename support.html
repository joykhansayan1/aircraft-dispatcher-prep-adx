What does the app include?

Hundreds of practice questions across all eight ADX domains, each with a detailed explanation; full-length timed mock exams; practice by topic and difficulty; flashcards; spaced-repetition review; an offline study library; and a readiness dashboard. Everything works offline.

Is a purchase required?

Yes. Full access requires a subscription (US$9.99/month or US$49.99/year, with a 3-day free trial on the yearly plan) or a one-time Lifetime purchase (US$99.99). Payment is charged to your Apple Account or Google Play account; subscriptions auto-renew unless canceled before the period ends.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open the app, go to Settings → Restore Purchases, and use the same Apple ID (iPhone, iPad) or Google account (Android) you used to buy.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. You can also use Settings → Manage Subscription inside the app.

How do I reset my study progress?

In the app, go to Settings → Reset all progress. All data is stored only on your device, so deleting the app also erases everything.

Do you collect my data?

No. All study data stays on your device. There is no account, no analytics, and no ads. See our Privacy Policy.

Are these official FAA questions?

No. Questions are written by our team from publicly available FAA references and exam objectives, plus sample questions the FAA itself publishes. We do not use secure or live test items. If you spot an issue, tap "Report a problem" on any question or email us.
